## Lane A: field (`fp-core/src/field/`)

- `gl64.rs`: `canonical`, `add`, `sub`, `neg`, `mul_wide`, `reduce128`, `mul`, `dot3`,
  `times_nr`, `from_u64`, `from_word`; exact C++ semantics, including the borrow/carry masks and
  the canonical last step.
- `ext.rs`: `F` and the cubic extension `E = F[X]/(X^3-2)` with the lazy-reduction product,
  inversion, powers, byte encodings and `theta`.
- `mle.rs`: `eq_table`, `eq_index`, `eq_eval`, `poly_eval`, `fold_pair`, `mle_eval`,
  `product_table`, `product_index`, `product_mle`, `product_dot`.
- Vectors: `scripts/cpp_vectors/field_vectors.cpp` (independent tool, includes `blocks/field`),
  **18,256 deterministic lines**; `fp-host/tests/field_vectors.rs` recomputes every line.
- `scripts/parity_field.sh` regenerates the vectors, requires a byte-identical fixture, then runs
  the Rust test.

## Lane B: hash (`fp-core/src/hash/`)

- `calls.rs`: `HashCalls { f2b, blk }`, `begin()` resets both (the `calls_begin` semantics of
  `cp_verify.cpp:2698-2700`), `total()` is the `q_V` sum.
- `blake2b.rs`: BLAKE2b compression `F`, `compress64`, `compress`, parameter block and first
  chaining values; `compress64` increments `blk`.
- `earlier.rs`: `f2b` compression with its own counter, `earlier_hash_c`, `H(num; w)` with the
  `num`/`l` high counter half and the 64-byte-digest initial state.
- `modes.rs`: `Digest`, `Tag`, incremental `Hasher`, Merkle leaves and vertices, `two_digests`,
  `run_value`, `chain_absorb`/`chain_call`/`chain_call_cells`, `work_test`, `test_coins`.
- `domains.rs`, `tweak.rs`, `th.rs`: domain numbers/predicates, `Tweak`, and `TH` under numbers
  10/11.
- `HashCalls` is carried in `StageState` and is also an explicit parameter of every hash call.
- Vectors and references:
  - `verifier/tests/known_answers/blake2b_f.py`: self-test (RFC 7693 “abc” + hashlib), 200
    regenerated F vectors;
  - `verifier/tests/hash_vectors.txt`: 44 `H` + 13 `TH` lines, checked again by
    `format_hash.py --hold`;
  - `blocks/hash/tests/mode_vectors.txt`: 58 lines, regenerated by `mode_vectors.py`;
  - RFC 7693 Appendix A `BLAKE2b-512("abc")`;
  - `scripts/cpp_vectors/hash_counters.cpp`: the same input sequence under
    `BLK_COUNT_HASH_CALLS=1`; counts `H 147/0`, `TH 49/0`, `BLOCKS 0/16`, `MIXED 5/2`
    match Rust.
- `scripts/parity_hash.sh` regenerates all four sources, compares the fixtures, and runs both
  Rust tests.

## Lane C: numbers, plan, accounting (`fp-core/src/numbers/`)

- `real.rs`: `Big` (limbs, add/sub/mul, Knuth Algorithm D `divmod`, `isqrt`, `pow`, shifts),
  `Real` (directed add/sub/mul/div/sqrt/pow, `cut`, `floor`, `ceil`, `top`, `log2`, `value`),
  `Bound` (failure flag, `surely_le/lt`, `error`), `bits_of`, `cut2`. `log2`/`exp2`/`floor` are
  `libm` calls; all decisions use integer comparisons.
- `setting.rs`: `Setting`, `RoundKind`, `ErrorKind`, `Scope`, `CountSource`,
  `setting_goldilocks()`.
- `codes.rs`: `CodeSize`/`Word`, `hidden_positions`, `tail_for`, `rho_of`,
  `johnson_list/count/johnson`, `at_distance`, `mca_error`, `positions`, `largest_m`,
  `powers_error`, `field_error(_nonzero)`, `sample_error`.
- `plan.rs`: `PlanRound`, `PricedGroup`, `PricedTable`, `PlanMessage`, the `Planned` verdict
  (`REFUSED`, `ABOVE_THE_TARGET`, `WORD`, `TWO_DISTANCES`), `capped()` and `whole_error()`.
- `accounting.rs`: `account()` with groups, merge/sort, `lists`, `closeness`, `by_work`, `near`,
  `small_union`, `planned_calls`, `on_a_word`, plus `compiled_level()`.
- Vectors:
  - `scripts/cpp_vectors/numbers_vectors.cpp`: **3,785** deterministic lines over `Big`, `Real`,
    `Bound` and `codes.h`; `fp-host/tests/numbers_vectors.rs` recomputes them.
  - `scripts/cpp_vectors/dump_plans.cpp`: links `verifier/src/cp_verify.cpp` compiled with
    `-DCP_VERIFY_AS_A_UNIT` and calls `cp_with_configured()` on the example configuration. It dumps
    23,265 lines (5.6 MB): `SETTING`, four plans (`key`, `entry`, `proof`, `all`), every round and
    message, and the C++ accounting of each. `fp-host/tests/numbers_golden.rs` rebuilds each
    `Planned` in Rust and compares the accounting field by field.
- The four `result-v1` numbers are recomputed by Rust, not copied:

```text
cut2(all.groups[0].all())              = 128.00   (least-round)
cut2(all.lists.bits)                   = 96.23    (least-round-without-work)
all.by_work                            = 38       (work-rounds)
key.closeness[1] + entry.closeness[1]  = 49       (commitment-assumed-rounds)
```

- All four C++ accountings (`key`, `entry`, `proof`, `all`) match the Rust ones on
  `formed/holds/needs_work/target/digest_bits/rounds/queries`, group count, first group
  bits/work/rounds/name/holds/list, `lists`, `closeness`, `by_work`, `near*`, `sum*`,
  `planned_calls`, `small_columns`, `small_union`, and `on_a_word`. Printed `f64` figures are
  compared after `cut2` (two decimals), as in C++.
- `scripts/parity_numbers.sh` regenerates both fixtures, requires byte identity, then runs both
  Rust tests.

## What S2a does not do (recorded for S2b/S3)

- `blocks/numbers/blocks.h` planners and `Plan`'s builder/`Cursor` are not ported in S2a: they
  take their inputs from the S2b shapes (`Sized`, `LinSized`, `WfSized`, ...). The accounting gate
  is satisfied by consuming the plans the C++ verifier itself makes, which exercises `Real`,
  `Bound`, `PlanRound` and `account()` on the real example.
- `kind_of_round`/`names_of_the_format` plan-name validation, `size_of` printing and the text
  rendering of `print_accounting` are S2b/S3; the numbers are already computed.
- `blocks/iop`, `blocks/frame` and the three verifier sub-proof checks remain S2b.
- S5/S6 (SP1/RISC0) untouched, per PM: only planned after S3 passes.

## Reproduce

```sh
cd rust-nostd-verifier
cargo build --workspace
cargo test --workspace
grep -rn "use std\|extern crate std\|static mut" fp-core/src/ || echo NO_STD_OK
./scripts/parity_field.sh
./scripts/parity_hash.sh
./scripts/parity_numbers.sh
./scripts/parity.sh
```

The parity scripts rebuild their C++ tools from the repository's own headers; no network is
needed after `cargo` has fetched the pinned `libm` crate. `Cargo.lock` is committed.
