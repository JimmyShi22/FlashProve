term	what it means in this appendix	the Lean's word
group	A set of tenants, opened with one main code and one mask code, on which a proximity test is run. Linear constraints are imposed on a group until its test begins.	`Statement.groups`; newGroupP, joinP, addRowsP; `SymbolComponent.groups`
tested, untested group	A group is tested once a proximity test has begun on it, untested before. A chain's last sub-protocol rejects if a group is untested, or a sampled tenant is in no tested group; the first two chains leave out one group, which the third chain tests.	`inRun`; untestedL, untestedButOwnL
linear constraint	A linear equation on the entries of tenants, with coefficients that the verifier computes and a target value. It is imposed on a group (after a fold: on the group's folded rows) and not checked there; the group's proximity test takes it as input. (A claim, by contrast, is gathered for its segment's sub-protocol `batch`.)	`OnBytes.Row`, `GroupRow`
seed; answer	A seed is a sampled point z with a sampled nonzero factor β. A tenant's answer y to a seed is sent by the prover and gives the linear constraint ⟨eq(z, ·), g⟩ + β·⟨θ, pad⟩ = y, where g is the tenant's entries outside the pads, the pad is a few entries reserved for that answer, and ⟨θ, pad⟩ weights them by the powers of a fixed element θ. A tenant's seeds and answers are its out-of-domain sample.	rounds "SEND seed", "SEND answer"; cutSeeds, sampleRow
masked sumcheck	A sumcheck on H + ε·S: the prover first sends the sum μ of a mask polynomial H, then ε is sampled, and the running claim starts at μ + ε·(the claimed sum). In each round the prover sends the round's polynomial without the one coefficient that the running claim fixes, and a challenge is sampled. No equation is checked round by round; one claim or one linear constraint is left.	rounds "PSC …", "FOLD …"; pscBeginAt, pscRound; complete, after, hear
batching	One element x is sampled; each of a list of claims or linear constraints is multiplied by a power of x fixed by its number, and all are added into one.	rounds "BAT", "BAT-sigma"; weightNext, addOne; batch; chiOf
claim on the committed table	A linear equation ⟨g ⊗ eq(ψ, ·), M⟩ + (a form in mask entries) = target on the matrix M that the table encodes.	`TensorRow`, `WsClaim`
stage	One level of a proximity test, given by a main code, a mask code and three numbers k, h, t. Every stage but the last has a fold of 1 + k rounds and a code switch of 2 rounds.	`Fmt.M1.Stage`
code switch	Two rounds: the prover sends new oracle messages and seeds are sampled; the prover sends the answers and positions are sampled. Earlier oracle messages of the chain are opened at these positions, and the opened values give linear constraints on the new tenants, imposed on a new group.	`K2.codeSwitchKind`; the proximity test's "the switch"; switchBehind
sealed chain	A chain whose last round is the round named SEAL.	chainEnd … sealed; sealRound
adopted	An oracle message of an earlier chain is adopted by a later one: it is entered in the later chain's verifier state under its own tree number and source. Rejected unless it has a tree to itself, has the declared shape, and was sampled in the earlier chain under the same shape and codes.	takeOver; adoptP, elsewhereP
array	A numbered datum of the layout. An oracle-message sub-protocol inside a segment marks the arrays in its list as committed.	arrays : Option (List Nat); commitOne
rows folded at a point; fold	A table's or a group's rows folded at a point γ: the one row Σ_r eq(r, γ)·row_r. A fold, which occurs at the close of a segment and in a proximity test, is a masked sumcheck of 1 + k rounds whose challenges form such a point. It takes one linear constraint on a group and leaves one linear constraint on the group's rows folded there, its kept tenants and two mask rows.	weights, eqTable; rounds "FOLD epsilon", "FOLD round"; foldD
base case	The two rounds that end a proximity test. The prover sends a value τ*, and λ is sampled; the prover sends in the clear the message and tail of w* + λ·w and of u* + λ·u (w, u: the last tenant's main and mask word; w*, u*: a masking tenant's), and t positions are sampled. The oracle message that contains both tenants is opened there. Checked: ⟨c, M0⟩ + ⟨d, M1⟩ = τ* + λ·τ (M0, M1: the two messages sent; c, d, τ: the linear constraint that is left), and at each position x the re-encoded symbol equals w*(x) + λ·w(x), and likewise for u, at x's place in the shorter word.	rounds "BASE lambda", "BASE"; baseDecide, baseChecks
product tree	Over 2^D leaves, the binary tree of their products. A claimed value of a level at a point z is moved one level down by a masked sumcheck whose summand is the product of the two children's values vL·vR, followed by one sampled element ξ: the next point is (ξ, r), the next claimed value (1 − ξ)·vL + ξ·vR.	level, stepDown, run; round "LINE"
shift	In a lookup argument: a field element (theta in the Lean code) that is sampled before the point α where the parameters say so; the padding values and the table's rows are functions of it.	`Face.shifted`; round "LKP shift"
key	Used in "key file" and as the first component of a column, and nowhere else: a committed table's `TableKey` is "its out-of-domain seeds and answers"; Product-tree lookup argument's keys are "looked-up values".
